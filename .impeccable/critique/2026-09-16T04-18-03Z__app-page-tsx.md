## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Mixed. The photography (real job-site/team photos), the government client roster (MINECO, MINFIN, IGSS, MINEX), and the Spanish construction-domain copy are genuinely grounded in this company. But the structural skeleton — a SectionHeading eyebrow+title formula repeated six times, alternating white/gray-50/navy background bands, a 3-column value-prop card grid, mission/vision cards — is a generic B2B-marketing-page template that any services company could drop unrelated content into unchanged. ## Overall Impression

The site is honest and content-accurate — nothing here contradicts the brief or invents information — and the accessibility fundamentals (focus rings, 44px touch targets, `aria-current`, `lang="es"`) are handled better than most first passes. But the page reads as a competent generic template wearing PROSERGUA's photos rather than a page built around what actually makes a B2G construction contractor credible: verifiable proof, not adjectives, and the government client roster undersold as an afterthought.

## What's Working

- Real, specific photography sourced from the company's own presentation, used consistently instead of stock imagery.
- The client roster (real ministry logos) is a legitimate, hard-to-fake trust signal for a B2G audience — when it's given room to matter.
- Accessibility basics: `aria-current`/`aria-expanded` on the nav, visible focus-visible rings throughout, 44px+ touch targets, `lang="es"` set at the root.

## Priority Issues

- **[P0] No B2G-specific credibility signals**: The homepage never states registration/NIT, insurance, or safety-certification specifics behind the "cumplimos las normativas de seguridad más estrictas" claim in the value proposition. Government evaluators need verifiable proof, not adjectives. **Fix**: add a credentials/track-record strip (years active, projects completed, certifications if PROSERGUA has documentable ones) before the Clients section. **File**: `data/company.ts`, `components/sections/About.tsx`. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify`.
- **[P1] Dead affordance in the Projects grid**: Thumbnails scale on hover like the clickable Service cards do, but have no link or click handler on the homepage preview — users expect a case-study detail and get nothing. **File**: `components/sections/Projects.tsx`. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`.
- **[P1] Trust peak buried, not recalled at close**: The Clients section (real government logos — the strongest trust signal on the page) sits second-to-last as small uncaptioned tiles, then the page closes on a generic inspirational quote instead of recalling that credibility. **Files**: `components/sections/Clients.tsx`, `components/sections/ContactCTA.tsx`. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout`.
- **[P2] Decorative blob undercuts institutional tone**: The `rounded-full bg-primary/90` circle in the Hero reads as generic SaaS decoration, in tension with the brief's explicit "no parezca una startup" instruction. **File**: `components/sections/Hero.tsx`. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable quieter`.
- **[P2] Mobile hero photo nearly erased**: The mobile hero uses a `bg-secondary/75` overlay over the real photo, muting the one differentiator (authentic job-site photography) that most visitors on phones see first. **File**: `components/sections/Hero.tsx`. **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`.

## Persona Red Flags
